# Rule sources

Accessed: 2026-07-22.

## Primary authorities

### Pokémon Trading Card Game Rules

- Publisher: The Pokémon Company International
- URL: https://www.pokemon.com/static-assets/content-assets/cms2/pdf/trading-card-game/rulebook/par_rulebook_en.pdf
- Indexed publication recency: approximately October 2025; the protected PDF endpoint did not expose a cover-version string to the local tooling.
- Used for: turn actions, first-player restrictions, Stadium rules, retreat, evolution, attack sequence, Weakness/Resistance, Pokémon Checkup, Special Conditions, Knock Outs, Prize cards, and terminology.
- Implemented from this source during this audit: first-player first-turn attack/Supporter gates; retreat restrictions; shared Stadium rules; Special Condition coexistence/removal; Poisoned, Burned, Asleep, and Paralysis Checkup primitives.

### Pokémon Trading Card Game Rules — Scarlet & Violet reference

- Publisher: The Pokémon Company International
- URL: https://assets.pokemon.com/assets/cms2/pdf/trading-card-game/rulebook/svi_rulebook_en.pdf
- Copyright/version context: 2023 Scarlet & Violet rulebook.
- Used for: glossary cross-checks covering Pokémon ex, Tera Pokémon ex, Technical Machines, Stadiums, VSTAR Powers, Special Conditions, retreat, and Sudden Death.

### Competitive Pokémon Rules and Formats

- Publisher: Play! Pokémon
- URL: https://play.pokemon.com/en-us/resources/rules/?category=tcg
- Used for: keeping tournament legality separate from mechanics, regulation-mark rotation, and locating current tournament resources.

### Pokémon TCG Errata

- Publisher: Play! Pokémon
- URL: https://play.pokemon.com/en-us/resources/documents/tcg-errata/
- Page last-update shown when accessed: 2024-11-07.
- Used for: Pokémon Tool classification and corrected text such as Potion's 30-damage healing and Rare Candy timing.

## Official exact-card sources

- Pokémon TCG Card Database: https://www.pokemon.com/us/pokemon-tcg/pokemon-cards/
- Arven SVI 166: https://www.pokemon.com/us/pokemon-tcg/pokemon-cards/series/sv01/166/
- Potion SVI 188: https://www.pokemon.com/us/pokemon-tcg/pokemon-cards/series/sv01/188/
- Switch SVI 194: https://www.pokemon.com/us/pokemon-tcg/pokemon-cards/series/sv01/194/
- Scarlet & Violet official checklist: https://assets.pokemon.com/assets/cms2/pdf/trading-card-game/checklist/svi_web_cardlist_en.pdf

The official database and checklist establish exact identity and printed metadata. The official errata page is authoritative where corrected text differs from older printings.

## Unresolved ambiguity and limitations

- The current protected rulebook PDF could be indexed and searched but not opened directly by the local browser tooling; its indexed text and the accessible official Scarlet & Violet rulebook were cross-checked.
- Tournament procedure for simultaneous win conditions and Sudden Death is not yet integrated into the engine.
- Card-specific rulings needed by future canonical decks must be added here with an official source before those cards become complete.
- Community databases may assist metadata import, but they are not primary rules authorities and do not make printed text executable.
