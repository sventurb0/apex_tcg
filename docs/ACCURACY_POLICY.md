# Accuracy policy

Catalogue availability and simulation accuracy are separate.

- Every source-dataset card may be browsed and used in a saved deck.
- Casual, incomplete and format-illegal decks may still be edited and saved.
- Printed metadata never implies executable behaviour.
- A game or simulation launches only when every exact printing has a `complete` implementation or a validated `generated` composition of trusted primitives.
- `partial` and `unsupported` cards produce a development report at launch; they do not make the Deck Builder unavailable.
- Special Energy restrictions are never approximated as unrestricted Basic Energy. In particular, Team Rocket's Energy remains unsupported until its attachment restriction, discard condition and dual two-Energy provision are exact.

Fictional cards remain test fixtures only. Official artwork, scans, logos and branded frames are not downloaded or bundled.
